# Decision and audit log - complete Turkish Open Logic edition

Date: 2026-08-14

## Publication object

1. The object is one complete Turkish edition of the fixed 722-file Open Logic
   content closure, not an evolving partial checkpoint.
2. It remains in the existing Turkish DOI lineage: concept DOI
   `10.5281/zenodo.21921844`; exact release DOI
   `10.5281/zenodo.21927145`.
3. The public surface has four files only: the complete linked reader, editable
   sources, evidence/provenance, and a SHA-256 manifest. The reader is file 00.
4. No genuine global Open Logic multilingual concept DOI is known. The release
   preserves `IsTranslationOf` intent and does not invent `IsPartOf`.

## Language and translation boundary

1. Turkish is maintained as Turkish. It is not a proxy for Uzbek, Uyghur,
   Azerbaijani, Kazakh, or another Turkic standard.
2. Reusable interlanguage observations are recorded in the hardened
   `TURKIC_INTERLANGUAGE_NOTEBOOK.md`; they are evidence infrastructure, not a
   claimed bridge language, grammar, or pilot reader.
3. Tokenized Open Logic locale terms are separated from productive Turkish
   suffix morphology. Unsafe joins were repaired with explicit boundaries or
   context recasting and replayed on settled bytes.
4. Frozen-source defects corrected locally are itemized in
   `SOURCE_ADVERSE_0722.md`. The English authority remains byte-untouched.

## Review and release boundary

1. Exact source/target identity, structural checks, model/agent review,
   successful compilation, extracted-text scans, all-page render inspection,
   and immutable hashes are the release gates.
2. Human or native-language review is neither required nor claimed.
3. Work is credited as performed at Floris's direction by “OpenAI 5.6 Sol,
   Ultra mode” (“OpenAI's current flagship model for complex work”).
4. The standalone repository path is the living Turkish publication mirror.
   Git commit/tag/release and Zenodo submission/readback are separate actions
   and must be evidenced after they actually occur.
5. Generated TeX intermediates, contact sheets, render scratch pages, caches,
   credentials, and `.git` internals are excluded from release archives.
